Why Voca

A dictation tool built for privacy, speed, and staying out of your way.

🔒

100% private

Transcription runs entirely on your Mac. No audio is ever uploaded — it even works with Wi-Fi off.

⚡

Fast & accurate

Whisper large-v3-turbo on the Apple GPU. The model stays warm, so dictation lands in about a second.

🧠

Learns your words

Teach it names, brands, and jargon, and fix recurring mistakes. Voca gets more accurate the more you use it.

✍️

Clean, formatted text

Automatic punctuation and capitalization, English-only output, and no capital when you dictate mid-sentence.

🔇

Noise-aware

An on-device voice-activity model ignores silence and background noise, so it never types junk.

🔋

Battery friendly

Event-driven — the mic only opens while you hold the key, and it uses ~0% CPU sitting idle.

Install in a minute

Download, drag, and grant two permissions. That's it.

1

Download & drag

Open Voca.dmg and drag Voca into your Applications folder.

2

Right-click → Open

The app is unsigned, so the first time, right-click Voca and choose Open.

3

Grant & go

Allow Microphone and Accessibility. The first launch downloads the models once, then it's fully offline.

To dictate: place your cursor anywhere, hold Right Option, speak, and release — your text is pasted right where you're typing.
